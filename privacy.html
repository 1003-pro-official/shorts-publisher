Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Shorts Publisher ("the app") is a personal tool operated by one creator for their own YouTube channel.

Data the app accesses

With the creator's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API scope youtube.upload only to upload videos, titles, descriptions and tags that the creator made, to the creator's own channel, and to set their publication time. It does not read, collect or analyze viewers' data, comments, or any other Google user data.

Storage

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the app, and are not shared with anyone.

Sharing

No Google user data is sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising or for training AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/1003-pro-official/shorts-publisher.
